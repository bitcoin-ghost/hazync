=== THE FINDING THAT MATTERS: risc0 uses ONE device ===

K=4 OOM'd at 36.9 GiB peak on a box with 92 GiB installed. That is not a memory shortage — the
second L40S was completely idle. risc0 binds device 0 unless CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES says otherwise,
so the VRAM ceiling is PER CARD, not total, and extra cards buy nothing by default.

Pinned, each card carries its own load:

  K=2 [gpu 0]  2.00 s/fold   peak 12,094 MiB
  K=2 [gpu 1]  2.00 s/fold   peak 11,742 MiB

=== BOTH CARDS SIMULTANEOUSLY (the number that counts) ===

  K=3 on gpu 0 AND K=3 on gpu 1, at the same time:
  6 folds in 7 s  ->  1.16 s effective per fold, 6/6 outputs produced, rc=0

Measured simultaneously, not inferred by adding two separate runs — running both cards at once could
have contended on CPU or PCIe, and this shows it does not meaningfully.

=== WHOLE-BOARD PROJECTION ===

  1 GPU,  K=3         2.00 s/fold
  2 GPUs, K=3 each    1.16 s/fold    ~1.7x

Multiply by (receipts - 1). The board grows continuously, so this cost RISES the longer the fold is
deferred — at ~2,500 blocks/hr the projection gains roughly an hour per day of delay.

=== CAVEAT ===

Folds here are over EARLY receipts (blocks 1-12), whose UTXO boundaries are small. A fold verifies two
receipts and checks the seam, so cost is flat in span — but per-fold VRAM at high-UTXO heights has not
been measured, and the K=3 ceiling sits close to the OOM point (36,868 vs 36,914 MiB). K=2 is the
safer setting for an unattended multi-hour run; K=3 buys ~20% and risks losing the level.

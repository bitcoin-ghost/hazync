## ⛔ Why more cards would not have fixed it

From the aggregate's own per-block accounting (`agg-history.log`), every block is three phases:

| block | segments | execution | worker wall | assembly | **serial floor** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 969,018 | 6,269 | 205.8 s | 573.2 s | 106.6 s | **312.4 s = 52 % of the gate** |
| 969,019 | 8,924 | 277.3 s | 859.8 s | 161.9 s | **439.2 s = 73 % of the gate** |
| 969,020 | 2,104 | 83.9 s | 211.7 s | 53.4 s | **137.3 s = 23 % of the gate** |

**`execution` and `assembly` run on the aggregate alone.** They are the serial floor: no number of
workers reduces them. For 969,019 that floor is **439.2 s of a 600 s budget before a single worker
does anything**.

The worker phase *does* scale, and measurably so — per segment per card it is near-constant:

```
969,018   6,269 segs / 18 cards = 348 per card   1.65 s each
969,019   8,924 segs / 19 cards = 470 per card   1.83 s each
969,020   2,104 segs / 22 cards =  96 per card   2.21 s each
```

So to fit 969,019's parallel work into the 160.8 s the serial floor left over would have taken
**~102 cards** — and that is an underestimate, because the join tree deepens with the fleet, so
`assembly` grows as cards are added and eats the very budget they are being rented to free.

⇒ **The lever for big blocks is the aggregate's execution and assembly, not the card count.**
Renting past ~25 cards buys progressively less and makes the serial floor slightly worse.

## ⚠ A pre-clock measurement that predicted nothing

The aggregate link was probed before the clock, as designed:

```
aggregate: hz-smoke-12 at 23446 Mbit/s (best of 4 candidate(s))
```

Sustained throughput during real proving was **34–52 Mbit/s** — three orders of magnitude below the
probe. The probe measures a bulk stream to idle workers; the real path is hundreds of thousands of
individual segment pushes interleaved with proving. The number is not wrong, it is answering a
different question, and it should not be read as a prediction of block wall time.

## Faults found, all introduced by me earlier the same day

1. **#564 — the feed died on every growth event.** `DashboardFeed.start()` spawns `stop` and `start`
   with `P_NOWAIT` and awaits neither. Harmless only while the stop did not work; once #562 made it
   effective, stop began killing the generation start had just launched. Six minutes with no
   telemetry, the frame reading `0/19 up`, and the spend frozen at $9.13 while 19 cards billed.
2. **#563 — `setsid` made streamers invisible to `ps | grep`,** the check a person reaches for when
   an orderly shutdown did not happen.
3. **The grid showed board blocks.** Sizing it to the session's six slots made gap-filling occupy
   the tip grid — more visible than before, not less. Fixed live mid-run.

⚠ All three were found by **running it**. None would have been caught by review, and the first two
were caused by a fix that was itself correct.
